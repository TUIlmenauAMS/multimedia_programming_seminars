Demo 3: Acoustic Scene — Recording Studio

A fully realized 3D recording studio with three acoustically distinct rooms: a control room, a live room, and an iso booth. Five real audio sources — guitar amp, drum kit, vocal mic, outboard rack, and acoustic piano — are placed spatially in the scene. Walk through the rooms and hear how distance attenuation, source directivity, and room acoustics combine into a believable spatial audio environment.

Navigate with left-drag to look, right-drag or scroll-wheel to walk. The acoustic room model switches automatically as you cross room boundaries — listen for the reverb character change when you step from the control room into the live room.

CONCEPT: Multi-source spatial mixing in Resonance Audio. All five sources contribute to the same third-order Ambisonic bus. Resonance Audio handles the spatial mix automatically — their relative levels at the listener are determined by position, distance attenuation (inverse square law: −6 dB per doubling of distance), and HRTF. No manual mixing is needed. The spatial mix is physically correct by construction.
CONCEPT: Guitar amp cardioid directivity. A guitar amplifier speaker radiates most of its energy forward — a cardioid or slightly narrower pattern. Walk behind the amp in the live room: the level drops noticeably. This matches the real experience of recording electric guitar in a studio. Resonance Audio exposes this via source.setDirectivityPattern(alpha, sharpness) — alpha=0.5 gives a cardioid pattern.
CONCEPT: Room switching and acoustic crossfade. Resonance Audio simulates one room at a time. When you cross a room boundary, the demo detects your position and calls resonanceAudio.setRoomProperties() with the new room's dimensions and material coefficients. A GainNode crossfade over 500 ms avoids an abrupt reverb change — the same technique used in production game audio engines. Listen carefully as you walk through the doorway from the control room into the live room.
CONCEPT: Distance attenuation — the inverse square law made audible. Walk from 1 m to 4 m from the drum kit. The level drops approximately 12 dB (two doublings of distance × 6 dB). This is not an abstraction — it is the inverse square law physically present in this simulation. Resonance Audio applies distance attenuation automatically for each source.
CONCEPT: Audio-reactive visuals via AnalyserNode. Each source has a dedicated AnalyserNode. Every animation frame, the demo reads the RMS energy of that source and drives the Three.js mesh emissiveIntensity and the co-located PointLight intensity. The source literally pulses with its audio energy — you see what you hear. Bloom post-processing (UnrealBloomPass) causes emissive meshes to glow proportionally to their energy.
