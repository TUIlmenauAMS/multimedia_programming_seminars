1. Visualizing Audio with AnalyserNode

The AnalyserNode is a special node that lets you inspect the audio signal without modifying it. It exposes two views of the signal:

  • Time domain — the raw waveform (amplitude over time), drawn as an oscilloscope.
  • Frequency domain — the spectrum (amplitude per frequency bin), drawn as a bar chart. This is computed using a Fast Fourier Transform (FFT).

The AnalyserNode sits in the signal chain as a pass-through tap — it reads the signal but does not alter it:

OscillatorNode → GainNode → AnalyserNode → AudioContext.destination

3. Key Concepts

The fftSize property controls the resolution of the frequency analysis. A larger FFT size gives more frequency bins (better frequency resolution) but requires more computation. The number of bins is always fftSize / 2.

Observe how a sine wave shows a single spike in the spectrum (one frequency), while a square wave shows many harmonics at odd multiples of the fundamental frequency — this is the Fourier series in action.
