About this demo
Ambisonics encodes multiple sound sources into a single 360° sound field — a property of the space, not the listener. The glowing sphere is that field: colour = acoustic energy arriving from each direction (dark = silent, bright = loud).
The head at the centre is you. Drag to rotate your head inside the fixed field — the audio panning changes but the field itself does not. This is the core difference from stereo panning.
